General (idx 40-129): Finnish morphology analogies (adjective-adverb, antonyms, comparative/superlative, participles, tense, person, plural) cannot be translated literally. Swedish equivalents with the same relation were chosen; position of the correct choice is unchanged. Distractors are other inflected/derived forms of the same word (plural/definite, comparative, superlative, noun forms, etc.) since Swedish lacks case endings.

31/36/32: setä -> "farbror", täti -> "faster" used consistently; isoisä -> "morfar", isoäiti -> "mormor".
40-59: Swedish adverbs formed with -t (same as neuter adjective). Items rebuilt with Swedish words close in meaning (e.g. hämmästyttävä -> förvånansvärd, valitettava -> beklaglig, vastahakoinen -> motvillig, mahdollinen/mahdollisesti -> sannolik/sannolikt, hiljainen -> ljudlös, onnekas -> lyckosam) because many Swedish adverbs equal the base adjective.

80-89, 120-129: Swedish verbs do not inflect by person, so pronouns were added ("han spelar", "vi spelar", "jag sjöng"); passives rendered as "det X-s"/"man X-ade"; conditional as "skulle + infinitive"; present participles end in -ande.
